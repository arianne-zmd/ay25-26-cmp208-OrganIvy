# ay25-26-cmp208-OrganIvy

## Title:

OrganIvy 

### Problem Statement:
Unnecessary pictures stored on phones contribute to the rise of electricity, heat and power used by data centers. This contributes to global warming, climate change and rising electricity prices. 

### Project Overview: 
Digital waste and the amount of data that needs to be stored is increasing everyday. Data is stored in data centers that use thousands of servers and these thousands of servers require an exuberant amount of energy and exude heat. This heat needs  to be dissipated with water coolers. 
People let unnecessary data build up on their phones not aware of the consequences that their unused data can cause. This app will serve as a platform where users can become conscious of the path their data leaves behind. This app will also gamify the act of deleting and managing photos, making it fun and effortless for the users.

### Project Scope: 
- Research the societal and environmental effects of digital waste, caused from the build up of photos, on mobile devices. 
- Design an android application that is pleasing to the eye and is easy to use. 
- Develop a mobile android application that scans the photo storage of a device for duplicated, large, or old images.
- Implement a two-step verification process before deleting important or sensitive photos.
- Implement gamified features such as  XP, levels, unlocking badges, receiving streak bonuses based on how much digital waste they clean,etc.
- Research estimated real-world benefits such as energy saved, carbon reduction, or storage optimized.
- Implement a password-protected or encrypted folder to store important images excluded from scans.
- Implement a method to track and display user clean-up progress and history over time.
- Test the application to ensure accuracy, data safety, and smooth performance.
